Almarzoq Academy

Teaching the foundations of fine art.

Al-Mrzoq Academy is an educational platform dedicated to individuals interested in fine arts. It teaches and disseminates the academic foundations of various art forms from a practical perspective, while also presenting the theoretical dimensions — the philosophy and history of art — for cultural and educational purposes.

Founded in 2024 by the artist Hasanin Al-Mrzoq, the academy features contributions from professors and specialists across art, architecture, medicine, marketing and programming. Each year it graduates hundreds of students from within Iraq and across the Arab world.
